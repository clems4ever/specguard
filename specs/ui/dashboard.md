## Behaviour

The report opens on a dashboard: a PASS/FAIL banner, coverage and pass/fail
stats, and every spec grouped by area — the at-a-glance view of the whole
catalog.

## Why

A traceability report is only a trust artifact if its overall state is legible
in one glance, before anyone drills into a single spec.
