## Behaviour

Opening a spec shows its rendered markdown behaviour, its covering tests, and
(when a run was ingested) its pass/fail state and any screenshots.

## Why

The detail view is where a reader confirms a behaviour is real: the words, the
tests that hold it true, and the visual proof.
